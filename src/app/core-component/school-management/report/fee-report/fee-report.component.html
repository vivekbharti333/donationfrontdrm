How this report is calculated

Total fee includes discounts and fines. Quarterly and half-yearly fees appear in their starting month; yearly and first-time school-life fees appear in April. Payments follow the installment month, regardless of payment date. Inactive, left, withdrawn, removed and deleted students are excluded.
